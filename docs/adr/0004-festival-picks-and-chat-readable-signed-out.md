# Festival Picks and chat are readable without sign-in

A Festival's Picks (with Nicknames) and its Festival chat are served to Visitors; only Picking and posting require sign-in. ADR 0001 accepted unauthenticated reads of a Room's Picks for frictionless link sharing; this extends that to a space with no link secret at all, because showing a Visitor a live, populated Festival is the point of the open space (ADR 0007). The trade: every Nickname and Pick is public to anyone who opens the site.
